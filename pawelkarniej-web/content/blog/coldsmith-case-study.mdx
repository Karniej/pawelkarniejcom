# Coldsmith: cold exposure and breathwork on iOS

**Product delivery case study — not an agent deployment result.**

Coldsmith is a focused wellness application for cold-exposure and breathwork practice. It combines guided sessions, timers, and repeat-use tracking without turning a simple routine into an overloaded platform.

## What I shipped

- Guided breathwork sessions
- Cold-plunge and ice-bath timers
- Streak and session tracking
- HealthKit integration
- Subscription paywalls and production release infrastructure

## Outcome

Coldsmith was released as a production iOS product. No retention, health, speed, or engagement result is published here because the repository does not contain evidence suitable for supporting one.
