# I sold Newsletterytics

I sold Newsletterytics.

This was the first app that made me money on the internet.

Five dollars. The first five dollars of a subscription I actually got.

I started working on it two years ago. Released it a little later. Grew it organically to the number one spot for the Beehiiv keyword.

Two years of history that was quite interesting.

I tried to work with the Beehiiv team. I tried to tell them they might want this application.

That's not my case anymore. Maybe the new buyer will do it. I don't know.

The app stays on the App Store. I just don't own it.

That's three apps sold now. Aividly, Rhava, Newsletterytics.

I still show the ones I sold. Same as Aividly. Someone bought it. That's the proof.

VidNotes, Teleprompter, Coldsmith, and YapperX stay because I still run them.

If you used Newsletterytics and you need the person who owns it now, that's no longer me.
