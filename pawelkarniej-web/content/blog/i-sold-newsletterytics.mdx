# I sold Newsletterytics

I sold Newsletterytics.

This was the first app that made me money on the internet.

Five dollars. The first five dollars of a subscription I actually got.

I started working on it two years ago. Released it a little later. Grew it organically to the number one spot for the Beehiiv keyword.

The product is simple. You connect your Beehiiv newsletter and you see the stats in the app and on a home-screen widget. Opens, clicks, subscribers, growth.

This year I added Mailchimp. That did not help the app grow as much as I wanted.

Two years of history that was quite interesting.

I listed it on TrustMRR. When I first listed it, the asking price was around 1.5 or 1.7 times ARR. The app grew a lot during the three weeks it sat there, so the close was closer to 1.1 times.

I sold it for $4,000.

The app had been doing about $200 a month. This month was closer to $300.

This is not life-changing money. It gives me a clearer path.

I tried to work with the Beehiiv team. I tried to tell them they might want this application.

That's not my case anymore. Maybe the new buyer will do it. I don't know.

Aivars called me out on [Fired by Robots](https://firedbyrobots.com/). He said this did not sound like focus.

He was right.

I am in a stage where I am cutting the extra timelines. [VidNotes](https://vidnotes.app/) and B2B automations stay. Newsletterytics was one of the branches I needed to close.

I thought that would take a week. After a week of doing it on top of client work, I think it will take a month.

I feel sentimental about the application. You cannot be sentimental in business. Business is business.

That's three apps sold now. Aividly, Rhava, Newsletterytics.

The app stays on the App Store. I just don't own it.

I still show the ones I sold. Same as Aividly. Someone bought it. That's the proof.

VidNotes, Teleprompter, Coldsmith, and YapperX stay because I still run them.

If you used Newsletterytics and you need the person who owns it now, that's no longer me.
