# Aividly: from AI media product to acquisition

**Product delivery case study — not an agent deployment result.**

Aividly combined AI image generation, video processing, and voice synthesis in one mobile creation product. The production challenge was not a single model call. It was turning those capabilities into a usable iOS product with rendering, storage, subscriptions, analytics, and App Store operations.

## What I shipped

- A React Native iOS application for AI video, image, and voiceover creation
- Cloud storage and media-processing infrastructure
- Subscription access and entitlement handling
- Analytics and production error handling
- App Store packaging and release execution

## Outcome

The product was built, released, operated as a real subscription application, and later acquired by its current owner.

No user-growth, speed, revenue, or engagement metric is published here because the repository does not contain evidence suitable for supporting one.
